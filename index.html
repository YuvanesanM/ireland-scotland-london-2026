Japan is wonderfully kid-friendly — clean, safe, punctual, and full of small delights. Here's what makes it easy & fun with Sana (9) and Tara (6).
Konbini magic
7-Eleven, Lawson & FamilyMart are everywhere · cheap tasty meals, onigiri, fried chicken, melon pan, puddings · great for picky eaters & quick breakfasts
IC cards for everyone
Get a Suica (Tokyo) / ICOCA (Kansai) each — tap on/off trains, buses & vending machines · kids' versions exist · or add Suica to phone wallets
Gachapon & vending
Capsule-toy machines everywhere · give each girl a small daily coin budget · drink vending machines on every corner (hot & cold)
Train etiquette
Quiet voices, no eating on local trains, line up on platform marks · rush hour (8–9 am) is packed — avoid with kids · trains are spotless & on time
Luggage forwarding (takkyubin)
Send big suitcases hotel-to-hotel (e.g. Kyoto → Tokyo) for ~¥2,000/bag · travel light on the Shinkansen with just a day bag · arrives next day
January weather ❄️
Cold & mostly dry, ~2–10°C · crisp blue skies, occasional light snow (esp. Kyoto) · pack thermals, gloves, hats, warm coats + comfy waterproof shoes · hand-warmers (kairo) from any konbini are a kid win
Shoes off indoors
Remove shoes at temples, some restaurants & ryokan · easy slip-on shoes save time · clean socks all round
Food for kids
Loads of wins: ramen, udon, gyoza, tempura, katsu, sushi conveyor belts, crepes, taiyaki · note: ask about veg/halal needs in advance — flag dietary requirements at booking
Toilets & amenities
High-tech heated toilets (a novelty for kids!) · clean public toilets everywhere · baby/kid facilities in malls & stations
Purikura & arcades
Photo-sticker booths & game arcades (claw machines!) in every entertainment district · a fun rainy-afternoon backup
Theme-park strategy
Arrive at opening, use ride-pass apps (Disney) / Express Pass (USJ), big rides first, breaks in the afternoon · bring a portable charger
Stay connected
Pocket WiFi or eSIM keeps maps + translation handy · Google Maps is excellent for trains · Google Translate camera for menus

Passes & cards
🎫 IC cards (Suica / ICOCA)
Get one each · tap on/off all city trains, subways & buses · top up at machines · also buys drinks & konbini snacks · add to phone wallet where possible
💴 JR Pass — probably skip
After the 2023 price rise, a 7-day JR Pass rarely pays off for just Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka · buy the single Shinkansen ticket instead · note the Nozomi isn't covered by the pass anyway
🧳 Travel light on the bullet train
Oversized bags need a reserved space behind the back row · easier: forward big suitcases Kyoto→Tokyo by takkyubin the day before & ride with day bags
📱 Navigation
Google Maps is excellent for Japanese trains — platforms, exits, live times · screenshot routes offline · station numbering (e.g. JY03) helps kids follow along
🚸 Kids: under 6 generally ride free on laps; ages 6–11 are usually half fare. Tara (6) & Sana (9) qualify for child fares — buy child IC cards or child tickets.
